This Privacy Policy explains how Bizel Global Investment ("we", "us"), the developer of the Ride Danfo game ("the Game"), handles information when you play the Game on Android or on our website. We built Ride Danfo to be played without an account, and we do not collect personal information from players.

1. Who we are

Developer: Bizel Global Investment (Victor Otite)
Contact: dtechx944@gmail.com
Website: https://ridedanfo.fun

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, or device identifiers. The Game has no sign-up or login.

3. Information stored only on your device

To remember your game settings and which content is unlocked, the Game saves a small amount of data in your device's local storage (for example, your selected pass level and game options). This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.

4. Voice and audio

Character voices are played from audio files included with the Game or produced by your device's built-in text-to-speech feature. The Game does not use your microphone and does not record audio.

5. Internet use and technical data

The Game uses an internet connection to load game files and a styling library (from cdn.tailwindcss.com). Like any website or online service, the servers that deliver these files may automatically receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser or device type, and the time of the request, and may keep it in routine server logs for security and reliability. We do not use this information to identify you, build profiles, or track you across apps or websites.

6. Advertising and analytics

The Game does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics software development kits (SDKs).

7. Purchases

The current version of the Game does not collect payment information. If paid features are offered in the future, payments will be processed by the store or payment provider (for example, Google Play), under that provider's own privacy policy. We will never receive or store your full card details.

8. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or any other purpose, because we do not collect it.

9. Data security

Because we do not collect personal information, we hold no player data on our servers. The Game and website are delivered over secure connections (HTTPS).

10. Data retention and deletion

Game data stored on your device stays there until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's app data in your Android settings, uninstalling the Game, or clearing your browser's site data for ridedanfo.fun. We have no player data to delete on our side, but you may contact us with any request at dtechx944@gmail.com.

11. Children's privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, the Nigeria Data Protection Act, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Because we do not collect personal information, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can contact us with any privacy question or request.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change significantly affects how we handle information, we will make that clear in the Game or on this page.
